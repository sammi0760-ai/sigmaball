Official Notice

Independent Tool Disclaimer & Public Notice

SigmaBall for Powerball is an independent statistical analysis and number-simulation tool. It is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Powerball, the Multi-State Lottery Association (MUSL), or any government or lottery authority. No tickets are sold and no real-money gambling is offered. Historical data is sourced from public datasets such as data.ny.gov.

All lottery drawings are independent random events. Statistical filters, frequency charts, and simulation algorithms are provided solely for analytical exploration, numerical research, and entertainment. SigmaBall for Powerball does not guarantee prizes or predict winning numbers.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent details regarding data sources, mathematical models, and privacy handling.

Q1 Is this an official Powerball or government lottery application?
No. SigmaBall for Powerball is an independent, third-party statistical analysis and number simulation utility. It is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Powerball, the Multi-State Lottery Association (MUSL), or any state or government lottery authority. It does not sell lottery tickets, accept wagers, or facilitate real-money gambling.
Q2 Where does the historical draw data come from?
Historical winning numbers and draw figures are compiled from publicly accessible open data repositories, specifically the New York State Open Data portal (data.ny.gov, "Lottery Powerball Winning Numbers: Beginning 2010"), for informational and educational research.
Q3 Does SigmaBall guarantee winning numbers or improve lottery odds?
No. Every official Powerball draw is a statistically independent random event. The odds of winning any prize remain identical for every valid combination (approximately 1 in 292.2 million for the jackpot). Statistical filters are exploratory analytical tools only and cannot predict future draw results or manipulate mathematical probabilities.
Q4 How is user data and Google AdMob advertising handled?

SigmaBall operates on a 100% on-device architecture. Your custom presets, generated sets, and preferences are stored locally in your device's isolated SQLite database and are never transmitted to developer servers.

To support free operation, the app integrates the Google AdMob SDK. AdMob may process advertising identifiers (AD_ID), approximate IP-based location, and diagnostic data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. For detailed disclosure, please review our English Privacy Policy.
