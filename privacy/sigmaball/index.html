1. Overview and On-Device Storage

Cho Yeon Planning respects your privacy. SigmaBall for Powerball is designed so that you can explore draw statistics and simulate number combinations without creating a user account or submitting personal profile information such as your legal name, home address, or payment card details directly to us.

Saved number sets, custom filter rules, simulation presets, and interface preferences (such as language selection) are stored locally on your device.

  • Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) or equivalent ad identifier (subject to your device and consent settings).

  • Ad impressions, ad clicks, interaction events, and app diagnostics/crash logs used for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, and fraud prevention.

You can manage or reset your Advertising ID and ad personalization preferences in your Android device settings. For more information on how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, please review Google’s Privacy Policy.

3. Public Lottery Data Source

Historical Powerball draw figures analyzed in the app are compiled from publicly available open datasets (such as NY Open Data at data.ny.gov) for informational, statistical, and simulation purposes only. Historical distributions and simulated combinations do not guarantee or predict future draw outcomes.

This application is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by applicable law in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
